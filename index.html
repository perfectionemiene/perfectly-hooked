Care Guide

Love It? Care for It.

Your handmade crochet pieces are crafted with care. With proper maintenance, they’ll stay beautiful for years to come.

Acrylic Yarn

Used for: Bags, Bag Charms, Earrings & Accessories

  • Spot clean whenever possible or hand wash in cool water.
  • Gently squeeze out excess water; do not wring or twist.
  • Reshape while damp and air dry flat. Avoid high heat & hanging wet.

Cotton Yarn

Used for: Clothing & Wearable Pieces

  • Hand wash in cool water with a gentle detergent.
  • Lay flat to dry and fold neatly for storage (avoid hanging).
  • Steam lightly if needed to remove wrinkles.

General Care Tips For Crochet Bags

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Avoid overloading your bag to maintain its shape.
  • Keep away from sharp objects that may snag the yarn.
  • For long-term storage, consider using a breathable fabric bag to protect from dust.
  • Stuff bags gently to maintain their shape when not in use.

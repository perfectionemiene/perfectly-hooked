Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, custom requests, and care.

Can I request custom colors or custom designs?

Yes! If you want an existing product in a color that isn't listed, we’ll make it for you at no extra charge. If you have a brand-new design in mind, reach out to us with reference photos or descriptions, and we’ll give you a personalized price quote.

How long will it take to complete and ship my order?

Since every piece is handmade, processing usually takes 3–7 business days depending on the size and complexity of the item. Once completed, your order is dispatched immediately, and tracking details are sent straight to your WhatsApp!

How do I place an order and pay?

Select your favorite items on our website and proceed to checkout, where payment will be processed through Paystack. Your order details will then be sent directly to our WhatsApp chat.

What is your return or exchange policy?

Because all items are custom-made or made-to-order, we do not accept returns or refunds once production has started. However, if your item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us immediately on WhatsApp and we’ll make it right.

How do I wash my crochet items?

We recommend spot cleaning or gentle hand washing in cool water with mild detergent. Always lay flat to dry! Check out our full "Love It? Care for It." Care Guide on the home page or download the digital care card sent to your WhatsApp after checkout.

Still have questions or ready to place a custom order? Don't hesitate to send us a message!!
